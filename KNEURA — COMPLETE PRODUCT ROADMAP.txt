KNEURA — COMPLETE PRODUCT ROADMAP
Core product vision

KNEURA is an evidence-governed AI layer for knee MRI that integrates with existing hospital imaging and reporting workflows, assists radiologists in detecting abnormalities, links every AI finding to imaging evidence, generates a report draft only from radiologist-approved findings, and distributes the verified result to the appropriate clinical users.

PHASE 5 — RADIOLOGIST VERIFICATION GATE 🔥🔥

This is the heart of your product.

KNEURA does not automatically turn AI predictions into report findings.

Every AI finding must receive:

     OR
REJECTED

Your current specification explicitly requires all 12 findings to receive a radiologist decision.
